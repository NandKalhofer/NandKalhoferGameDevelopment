CLUTCH PATCH

Overview

What’s that?! A pimple, right before you have to go on stage at the biggest streamer event? Find the new pimple patches before a fan finds you! Sneak, hide and solve puzzles, all while your Twitch chat tries to help or sabotage your plans.


My first project for a client, namely, L’Oréal Garnier. We started by collecting a lot of data and researching how they could enter the gaming industry. This lead to a 70 page research presentation, flowing into a proposal for a game and a marketing campaign. For the second part of the semester, we developed a prototype for a stealth game, connected to the Twitch API for Unity. Through this connection, L’Oréal would be able to do a campus tour around the Benelux and bring a streamer with them, that could interact with their chat during a sponsored stream.
