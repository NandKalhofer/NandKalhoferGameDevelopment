03 / Client Project, Game Design, Marketing

CLUTCH PATCH

Overview

What’s that?! A pimple, right before you have to go on stage at the biggest streamer event? Find the new pimple patches before a fan finds you! Sneak, hide and solve puzzles, all while your Twitch chat tries to help or sabotage your plans.


My first project for a client, namely, L’Oréal Garnier. We started by collecting a lot of data and researching how they could enter the gaming industry. This lead to a 70 page research presentation, flowing into a proposal for a game and a marketing campaign. For the second part of the semester, we developed a prototype for a stealth game, connected to the Twitch API for Unity. Through this connection, L’Oréal would be able to do a campus tour around the Benelux and bring a streamer with them, that could interact with their chat during a sponsored stream.


My role

Throughout the researching part I functioned as marketing director and halfway through also took over as the point of contact with the client. For the game development I was assigned to be assistant team lead and game designer. Once the game design was written down and decided on, I also filled in as gameplay programmer and focused on the enemy ai.

What I learned

As my first client project, I learned the importance of clear communication as our client had specific expectations that did not align with our assignment. Sometimes you have to stand your ground on what your team can do and this was definitely a good exercise. The client turned out very satisfied, which was in turn a very satisfying result for the team.

It opened my eyes to the possibilities within the industry and its connection to more unlikely partners. These partners bring new challenges, but also great opportunities and that was very valuable to experience first hand.
