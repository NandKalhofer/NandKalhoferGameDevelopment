ANIMALS ON SET

Overview

You are the star! Make sure everyone knows and steal your friends’ spotlight in front of the camera. Grab the right outfit, get in frame and pose, but be careful because your rivals will try to punch their way to fame as well. In this chaotic, party game, there is only one rule; Out of frame, out of fame!


For a whole semester I worked as the team lead on Animals on set, a party game, inspired by Party Animals. The team was made up of 5 other students, 2 programmers and 3 artists. It was my second time working in a group with different profiles.

My role

I shine in group projects and truly enjoyed the process of making this game. Other than team lead, my main jobs were game design and programming. However, since I have a fairly all-rounded profile, art direction was also part of my responsibilities, together with our art lead.
