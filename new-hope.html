NEW HOPE

Overview

After the fall of society, it only took a few months for nature to start reclaiming earth. However, in its cruelty, you can see some beauty, something the survivors will appreciate as they find their cities all dressed up by Mother Nature.



New Hope is a Unity project I made for my Game Art course. It involved Unity shader graph, Blender and optimisation. The requirements were to make an environment, that is both visually impressive and optimised, using shaders and skills taught in previous courses. All models, except the cars, were made by myself.

Breakdown


Here is a short breakdown of the progress. Since it was a still shot for the final assignment, I made sure to have good composition, however it also felt important to make sure it would be useful in a game where you can walk around. Therefore, I dressed up the ground plane some more, to simulate an actual game scene.

What I learned

During this project I lost track of time during the blockout and had to work rather fast on the other chapters of production. However, this tied nicely into the intention of this assignment, which is to work smarter, not harder. Shaders should make the work better, but also lift some of the workload, something that I desperately needed.


Not only did I have to work smarter for my time, but Unity optimisation was definitely something that intrigues me. Below you can see that by optimising it as much as possible, I managed to pull down the batches and up the FPS. Adding this to my skills will come in handy in every project I aid in from now on.
